# Impeccable Reference: Bolder & Quieter Steering

## 1. Bolder (`/impeccable bolder [target]`)
**Purpose:** Rescues timid, washed-out, or generic "AI template" UI.
- **Scale:** Increase headline contrast and size steps.
- **Contrast:** Push surface-to-text contrast ratios to $\ge 7:1$.
- **Decisive Palette:** Replace pale pastel washes with strong, saturated primary accents or high-contrast dark accents.
- **Form:** Strengthen border definitions, apply deeper layered shadows, or introduce bold editorial typographic treatments.

---

## 2. Quieter (`/impeccable quieter [target]`)
**Purpose:** Reduces cognitive overload, visual noise, and cluttered aggression.
- **Border Reduction:** Strip unnecessary borders; use subtle background shifts or whitespace dividers instead.
- **De-escalate Contrast:** Soften harsh secondary elements down to balanced muted values.
- **Whitespace:** Expand grid gaps and section margins by 25–50%.
- **De-clutter:** Hide tertiary actions behind overflow menus or secondary drill-downs.
